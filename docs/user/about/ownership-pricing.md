# Ownership & Pricing

Skelenote is free. Not "free with limits"—free.

---

## What's Free (Everything)

- Full desktop app (macOS, Windows, Linux)
- Full mobile app (iOS, Android)
- All features: encryption, AI, sync, export
- Local network sync across your devices
- Cloud sync across the internet
- Unlimited notes, tasks, projects
- Full export (Markdown, JSON, HTML, PDF)
- All future updates

---

## Why Free?

Skelenote is open source under the Apache 2.0 license. You can inspect the code, verify our encryption, and fork it if you want.

We think privacy software should be auditable and accessible to everyone. Your notes app shouldn't profit off of you.

---

## No Lock-In

- Your data is always yours
- Export everything as Markdown, JSON, HTML, or PDF
- Import from Notion, Obsidian, or plain Markdown
- Switch to offline-only mode anytime
- Fork the code if you want

---

## The Model

Our philosophy is simple:

1. **The app is free** — Every feature, no gates
2. **Your data stays yours** — Encrypted, exportable, portable
3. **No tricks** — We don't sell your data, show ads, or lock you in

That's the whole pitch.
