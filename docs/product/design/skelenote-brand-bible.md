## II. Visual Identity: "Cozy Rationalism"

Our design language is a direct response to the "Cold Blue" aesthetic of Silicon Valley SaaS. If Linear is an industrial warehouse, Skelenote is a study with a fireplace.

### The Aesthetic

We combine the high-density efficiency of a code editor with the warmth of a physical notebook.

- **The Palette:** We strip away the clinical blues and replace them with earth tones.
  - **Ember** (`#B85C50`): A warm terracotta used for primary actions and focus rings.
  - **Sage** (`#5E8C61`): A grounded green signaling success and completion.
  - **Canvas** (`#FAFAFA`): A soft off-white background that reduces eye strain, avoiding the harshness of pure white.
- **The Typography:** We use **Carbon** (`#18181B`) and **Graphite** (`#52525B`) text—never pure black—to soften the contrast and make long reading sessions comfortable.

### The Interface

* **High Density, Low Noise:** We respect screen real estate. Information is packed efficiently with tight gaps and minimal padding, but the interface recedes until needed.
- **Hover-Reveal:** Secondary actions only reveal themselves on hover to reduce visual noise.
- **Depth without Weight:** We avoid heavy drop shadows, using subtle 1px borders (`#E4E4E7`) to define hierarchy. It feels substantial, not floaty.

## III. Strategic Positioning: Your Key, Your Data

Skelenote establishes a new paradigm for personal data: **Encryption is sovereignty.**

While competitors hold your data hostage behind accounts and subscriptions, we give you the key—literally. Your 24-word Skeleton Key is the only thing that unlocks your vault. Not us. Not a server. You.

### The Concept: Encrypted Everywhere

Deep work happens in the **Digital Study**—alone, focused, and private. When you sync, your data travels encrypted with keys only you control.

- **The Principle:** End-to-end encryption means your data is private regardless of where it travels. The server sees ciphertext. Only your devices see plaintext.
- **The Mechanic:** Whether syncing over your local network (Hearth) or across the internet (Courier), data is encrypted with XChaCha20-Poly1305 before leaving your device.
- **The Feeling:** The freedom of syncing anywhere with the security of knowing nobody—not even us—can read your data.

### Two Paths, Same Privacy

- **Hearth** — Local network sync for devices in the same space. Fast, direct, no internet required.
- **Courier** — Cloud relay for devices anywhere. Same encryption, same privacy, global reach.

### The Differentiator

Most tools encrypt data "at rest" on their servers—meaning they hold the keys. We encrypt data **before it leaves your device**, with keys that never leave your control. The difference? We couldn't read your notes if we wanted to.
