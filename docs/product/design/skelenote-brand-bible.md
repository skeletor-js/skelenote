## III. Visual Identity

Our design is warm and dense—the opposite of cold SaaS blue.

### Palette

- **Ember** (`#B85C50`): Primary actions, focus rings
- **Sage** (`#5E8C61`): Success, completion
- **Canvas** (`#FAFAFA`): Background (soft, not harsh white)
- **Carbon** (`#18181B`): Primary text
- **Graphite** (`#52525B`): Secondary text

### Interface Principles

- **High density, low noise** — Tight gaps, minimal padding, interface recedes until needed
- **Hover-reveal** — Secondary actions appear on hover
- **Borders over shadows** — Subtle 1px borders define hierarchy

## IV. Strategic Positioning

**Encryption is ownership.**

Your 24-word Skeleton Key is the only thing that unlocks your vault. Not us. Not a server. You.

### How Sync Works

- **Local sync**: Devices on the same network sync directly
- **Cloud sync**: Devices anywhere sync via encrypted relay

Both use end-to-end encryption. We can't read your data either way.

### The Differentiator

Most tools encrypt data "at rest" on their servers—they hold the keys. We encrypt **before data leaves your device**, with keys that never leave your control.
